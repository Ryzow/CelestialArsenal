Celestial Arsenal brings cosmic-themed hybrid weaponry inspired by stars, meteors, and celestial forces into the world of Terraria!

✨ Key Features

- Dual-Class System (Melee/Magic): Cosmic weapons that combine close-range combat with the devastating power of celestial magic.
- Comet Sword (Phase 1): An early-game cosmic blade that summons a rain of comets from the sky with every swing. Crafted using Meteorite Bars and Fallen Stars.
- Celestial Comet Sword (Phase 2): A Hardmode evolution featuring a powerful Right-Click ability that summons a massive meteor to devastate enemies across a wide area! Upgraded at a Mythril or Orichalcum Anvil using a Comet Sword, Souls of Light, and a Unicorn Horn.

☄️ Harness the Power of the Cosmos!

Master the power of stars and meteors, unleash devastating celestial attacks, and obliterate your enemies!
