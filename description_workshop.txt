[h1]Celestial Arsenal[/h1]
Unleash the power of the cosmos in Terraria! [b]Celestial Arsenal[/b] introduces a new line of hybrid [b]Melee / Magic[/b] weaponry forged from fallen stars, meteorites, and celestial energy.

[h2]✨ Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hybrid Melee/Magic Class:[/b] Weapons benefit from both Melee and Magic stat bonuses, allowing flexible builds and devastating combos.
[*][b]Multi-Phase Evolution:[/b] Upgrade your cosmic blade as you progress into Hardmode to unlock catastrophic new abilities.
[/list]

[h2]⚔️ Weapons Included[/h2]

[b]1. Comet Sword (Phase 1 - Pre-Hardmode)[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Damage Type:[/b] Melee / Magic
[*][b]Ability:[/b] Calls down glowing cosmic comets from the sky toward your cursor on every swing, inflicting [i]Frostburn[/i] and [i]On Fire![/i] on impact.
[*][b]Recipe (Iron / Lead Anvil):[/b] 15x Meteorite Bar + 10x Fallen Star
[/list]

[b]2. Celestial Comet Sword (Phase 2 - Hardmode)[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Damage Type:[/b] Melee / Magic
[*][b]Left-Click:[/b] Rains down high-velocity cosmic comets at rapid speed.
[*][b]Right-Click (Special):[/b] Consumes Mana to summon a [b]Giant Meteor[/b] from the heavens that obliterates enemies on impact!
[*][b]Recipe (Mythril / Orichalcum Anvil):[/b] 1x Comet Sword + 12x Soul of Light + 2x Unicorn Horn
[/list]
