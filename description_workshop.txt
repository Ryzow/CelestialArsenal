[h1]Celestial Arsenal[/h1]
A cosmic sword that is both melee and magic. Swing it to call comets down from the sky, then upgrade it in Hardmode to charge a giant meteor with a right click.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Starfall Blade and Starfall Lance[/b]: pre-boss weapons that release homing stars.
[*] [b]Starfall Set and Starfall Charm[/b]: early game armor and accessory for the melee/magic hybrid class.
[*] [b]Comet Sword[/b]: a pre-Hardmode sword. Each swing calls a fiery comet down toward your cursor.
[*] [b]Comet Sword Awakened[/b]: the Hardmode upgrade. Two comets per swing, plus a charge attack.
[*] [b]Giant meteor[/b]: hold right click to charge, and the meteor falls on its own once fully charged. It explodes in a large area and sets enemies on fire.
[*] [b]Melee/magic hybrid damage[/b]: benefits from both melee and magic bonuses, such as emblems, armor and buffs.
[*] [b]Mana and cooldown[/b]: comets cost a little mana, the giant meteor costs more and has a cooldown shown as a buff.
[*] [b]Glowing sprites[/b]: the fiery and crystal parts of the blade stay bright in the dark.
[*] [b]Screen shake[/b] on explosions, which can be turned off in the client config.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Left click[/b]: swing the sword and call comets from the sky.
[*] [b]Right click (hold)[/b]: raise the sword and charge. Releasing early cancels the charge. Awakened form only.
[/list]
